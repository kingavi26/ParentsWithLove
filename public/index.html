How it works

Every answer goes through six steps

A research-based AI system with built-in, redundant self-checks — not a generic chatbot bolted onto a search engine.

1 · Listens

Takes in what's happening, plus your kids' ages and history.

2 · Checks the research

Grounds the answer in pediatric and child-development research — the same evidence base every time, not improvisation.

3 · Filters for fit

Customizes the advice to your child's age and your family's specific patterns — never one-size-fits-all.

4 · Double-checks

Runs a second, independent review before anything is sent — a redundant self-check that catches mistakes before you see them.

5 · Responds with warmth

No judgment, no shame — just something useful you can try.

6 · Remembers, on your terms

Picks up next time where you left off. Edit or delete anytime.
