How it works

Every answer goes through six steps

It's not a generic chatbot bolted onto a search engine.

1 · Listens

Takes in what's happening, plus your kids' ages and history.

2 · Checks the research

Grounds the answer in pediatric and child-development guidance.

3 · Filters for fit

Matches advice to your child's age and your specific situation.

4 · Double-checks

Reviews the answer before it's sent — never guesses or invents.

5 · Responds with warmth

No judgment, no shame — just something useful you can try.

6 · Remembers, on your terms

Picks up next time where you left off. Edit or delete anytime.
