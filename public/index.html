Disclaimer

Parenting Help Chat uses an AI model to write its replies. AI-generated answers can be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong — even when they sound confident. Always use your own judgment.

This bot doesn't provide medical, psychological, or legal advice, and talking to it doesn't create a doctor–patient, therapist–client, or other professional relationship. It's meant to help you think through everyday parenting questions, not to diagnose, treat, or replace your child's pediatrician, a licensed therapist, or another qualified professional. Check anything important with one before acting on it.

If your child — or anyone — is in immediate danger or a crisis, don't use this chat. Call your local emergency number (911 in the US) or a crisis line right away (in the US, call or text 988).

How your data is handled: what you type, and the facts this app remembers about your family (like a child's name/nickname and age), are sent to OpenAI's API to generate replies and to figure out what to remember. OpenAI doesn't use this to train its models, but does retain it briefly (per its own policy) for abuse monitoring. Only your own account can see your family's data day-to-day; the site's administrator can also view account data through an internal dashboard for support and moderation. See the privacy policy for the full picture, including how to edit or delete what's remembered.

You're always the one responsible for decisions about your child. This tool is provided as-is, with no warranty, and its creator isn't liable for actions taken based on its replies.
