What this page is

pwl7's replies are guided by a written parenting framework (love-first, understand-before-guide, warm boundaries, and so on) built around established child-development ideas — attachment theory, authoritative-parenting research, emotion-coaching research, and positive-discipline research. When a reply below a topic like tantrums, bedtime, screens, sibling conflict, or discipline shows a "Based on" line, it's pointing you to the real organizations whose published guidance covers that general topic.

What this isn't: a citation for that exact sentence. pwl7 doesn't look up a specific study or article before writing each reply, so these are topic-level pointers to reputable organizations, not a bibliography entry for a specific claim. If a particular answer matters for a real decision, please follow up with your pediatrician or a licensed professional — the assistant will tell you as much whenever a conversation touches on something outside general parenting guidance (safety, abuse, or sexual topics, for example).
